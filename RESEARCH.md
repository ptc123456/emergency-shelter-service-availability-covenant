## STAGE 1
- **Objective/users:** Issue a historical, event-scoped receipt for what an official emergency-management update said about a named shelter at a named publication time. Public emergency-response archives may consume it; it is never a current availability, routing, capacity, or admission signal.
- **Trust/GenLayer/mechanism:** A single evolving update page contains several timestamped sections, scheduled openings, actual-open statements, and later closures. Independent validators must bind event, section timestamp, facility, access class and publication-state verb; a current fetch of old text does not make the shelter currently open. Output includes `authority_match`, `section_time_match`, `facility_match`, `access_class`, `published_state`, `evidence_state`, and `HISTORICALLY_LISTED_OPEN/HISTORICALLY_LISTED_CLOSED/UNKNOWN` under the sealed event/section only.
- **Concrete official HTML fixture, checked 2026-09-21:** Orange County, Florida's [Idalia Updates](https://newsroom.ocfl.net/2023/08/idalia-updates/) page has an August 29, 2023, 2:00 p.m. section saying shelters “are now open”, naming Barnett Park and South Econ Recreation Center under `General Population Shelters (Pet Friendly)`. Its August 30, 2023, 10:30 a.m. update says general-population shelters are now closed and names the same two facilities. The August 28 section says they *will open* on August 29; it is a scheduled-state counterexample, not proof of actual opening. The exact positive receipt is `Idalia / August 29 2:00 p.m. / Barnett Park / GENERAL_POPULATION / PET_FRIENDLY / HISTORICALLY_LISTED_OPEN`; the later section returns historical closure, never current status.
- **Actors/evidence/validator:** manifest owner, assessor, reader; one allowlisted county page, sealed section anchor/timestamp and content hash. Validators independently refetch and extract full tuple. Fetch time and hash identify the observed page revision; the *authority-authored section timestamp* is the sole as-of basis. Caller-provided retrieval time cannot establish authority freshness.
- **Closest/differentiation:** Security Contact Availability Covenant and Evidence Freshness Gate. Inherited: availability wording. Material difference: event-section temporal reconciliation of scheduled/open/closed statements and access category for a named emergency shelter. Difference high; new value is an archival state-transition receipt, not live routing.
- **Risks/feasibility:** updates may be edited, time zone is local Orange County time, and archives may retain contradictory sections. Seal exact section and revision; missing timestamp, hash drift or conflict => `UNKNOWN`. No live safety reliance. Build must probe current GenVM HTML retrieval, not presume it here.
- **Acceptance:** official dated positive/open and subsequent closed evidence, scheduled-opening counterexample, no current-status assertion or unsupported freshness claim.

## STAGE 2
- **Model/lifecycle:** `EventManifest`, `FacilityProfile`, `SectionAssessment`; `DRAFT -> SEALED -> ASSESSED -> SUPERSEDED`.
- **Invariants/storage:** immutable authority/event/facility/section time/hash; fixed access and published-state enums; `HISTORICALLY_LISTED_OPEN` requires the *same section* to say actually open and name the facility and access class. `WILL_OPEN` is not `OPEN`; an August 30 closed section does not rewrite the August 29 receipt. Capacity/admission/current availability are always `NOT_ASSERTED`.
- **API/auth:** `create_event`, `register_facility`, `seal_section`, `assess_section`, `supersede_section`, `get_assessment`, `read_historical_listing`. Owner manages manifests; public assess/read; strict URL, hash, section-time, event, enum and replay checks. No `is_open_now` endpoint.
- **Prompt/EP:** source is delimited/untrusted; leader and validators independently extract the same bounded tuple; exact consequential-field comparison; explanation display-only.
- **Failures/retry/replay/appeal/timeout:** unavailable/changed/missing section or ambiguous time => `UNKNOWN`; identical replay idempotent; changed official revision creates child assessment; timeout preserves state.
- **Tests/E2E:** Idalia Aug 29 Barnett Park open and pet-friendly; Aug 30 same facility closed; Aug 28 scheduled-not-open; special-needs category mismatch; wrong event/facility/section, invented capacity or current-open claim, hostile text, malformed output, disagreement, auth, replay and readback.
